How LaunchPad Works

A structured framework to eliminate founder guesswork before writing code or spending capital.

Phase 1

Define Your Idea Parameters

Input your startup concept, addressable audience, industry vertical, and capital runway. Our system applies structured domain models tailored to your specific constraints.

Phase 2

Algorithmic Feasibility & Positioning

Receive a comprehensive diagnostic breakdown: composite feasibility score, strengths, critical vulnerabilities, risk mitigation protocols, and a hyper-targeted buyer persona.

Phase 3

Execute the 7-Day Sprint

Track your MVP build and check off daily deliverables from customer validation to public launch. All progress persists securely in your browser's local storage.
